# My Favorite Movie: The Notebook

## Overview
**The Notebook** (2004) is a *romantic drama* directed by Nick Cassavetes and based on the novel by Nicholas Sparks. It follows Noah and Allie, two young people from different backgrounds who fall in love in the 1940s.

## Why I Love It
- Emotional and heartfelt story
- Great chemistry between the lead actors
- Beautiful scenery and cinematography
- A love story that lasts across many years

## Main Characters
1. **Noah Calhoun**, played by Ryan Gosling
2. **Allie Hamilton**, played by Rachel McAdams
3. **Older Noah**, played by James Garner
4. **Older Allie**, played by Gena Rowlands
